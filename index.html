Connection settings (if friends can't connect)

Many home, school, and phone networks block direct connections between players. A relay (TURN) server gets around that. Make a free account at metered.ca (TURN server) or expressturn.com, then paste its details here. It works if either the host or the joiner has one, but both is best.

A bigger wrapping world. Rare nodes are simply rare, anywhere on the map.

  • Left thumb drives, right thumb aims and fires. On desktop use WASD and the mouse.
  • Every augment has its own shape on a tank, so you can read a mini boss before you fight it.
  • Lasers and particles fight on their own. Aim toward what you want them on; aim away and they let go.
  • Amber triangles are wild. They attack everyone, including mini bosses.
  • Your base collects drops in its range. Upgrade HP, defense, and range in its menu.
  • Dashed circles are safe zones, and the yellow shuttles connect them.
  • Inventory (I) works anywhere: swap parts between your tank, cargo, and drone bays, and pick which button fires each augment.
  • Drones collect drops. Put an augment in their bay and they fight with it at 25% strength. Right-click to send them at something.
  • The map has eight regions around the hub. Capture a region's beacon by holding its ring for 12 seconds; it pays out and defends you.
  • World events pop up every few minutes: convoys, meteor showers, rifts, and sieges. Contracts (C) give you goals and rewards.
  • Build turrets, extractors, a refinery, and a repair pylon from your base or inventory. H teleports you home after 3 seconds standing still.
  • Every zone has its own boss, tougher in each zone: from the Elder Thornback in the Meadow to the Hollow King in the Void. Bosses drop big loot and return after a few minutes.
  • Legendaries: Singularity (zone bosses, always from zones 7-9), Orbital Lance (supply convoys), Particle Engine (anomaly shapes). Rift horrors can drop any of them.
  • The world has nine biome zones, each harder and richer than the last: Meadow, Dunes, Ocean, Tundra, Marsh, Caverns, Volcano, Storm, and Void. Each has its own hazards and enemies.
  • Zones drop essence (colored diamonds). At your base, spend it to infuse an augment with that zone's power: frost chills, scorch burns, venom poisons, and more.
  • Visit a zone's outpost to unlock it for warping (G) from any safe zone. Bases are heavy but movable: push, grapple, or tow them.
  • Enter opens chat. P opens the party menu. Party members can't hurt each other, and bases leave them alone.
  • Anyone outside your party can shoot you. Your base fires on a player for 30 seconds after they attack it.
